About Aura Music Institute

Aura Music Institute was born from a simple belief: music has the power to transform lives.

We are a community of learners, teachers, and performers united by our love for music. Whether you are picking up an instrument for the first time or refining your craft, you will find a home here.

  • Classical foundations with contemporary techniques
  • Individual attention with ensemble experiences
  • Theory with practical performance
  • Discipline with joy
